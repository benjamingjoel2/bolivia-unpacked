Santa Cruz de la Sierra is Bolivia's largest city, at about 400 m in the eastern lowlands. Its Viru Viru International Airport is the country's other main gateway after La Paz. Starting a trip here lets you begin low and gain height gradually.

## The Jesuit Missions of the Chiquitos

East of Santa Cruz, a group of mission towns founded by Jesuits in the 17th and 18th centuries is on the UNESCO World Heritage list. The churches mix European and local styles and are still in use. Towns on the circuit include San Javier, Concepción, San Miguel, San Rafael, Santa Ana and San José de Chiquitos. Distances are long. A full circuit by road usually takes several days.

## Samaipata and Amboró

Samaipata, a small town at about 1,650 m west of Santa Cruz, sits below El Fuerte de Samaipata, a carved rock site on the UNESCO list. It is also a base for walks on the edge of Amboró National Park, including cloud forest with giant ferns.

## Health

All of Santa Cruz department falls inside CDC's recommended area for yellow fever vaccination. See [entry and health](/guide/entry-and-health/).
