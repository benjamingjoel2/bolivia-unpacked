Lake Titicaca lies on the border between Bolivia and Peru. Its surface is at roughly 3,810 m. The level changes with the seasons and from year to year.

## Copacabana

Copacabana, at about 3,841 m, is the main town on the Bolivian shore. Its basilica and the Cerro Calvario viewpoint are the main sights. Boats leave from here for Isla del Sol. The road from La Paz crosses the Strait of Tiquina by a short barge crossing for vehicles, while passengers cross in small boats.

## Isla del Sol and Isla de la Luna

Isla del Sol has Inca-period ruins, terraces and walking trails, with paths climbing to around 4,000 m. Access to parts of the island has been restricted at times because of local disputes. Check the current situation with your operator before you go. Isla de la Luna is smaller and visited less often.

## Fitting it into a route

- **From La Paz**: Copacabana is a few hours by road. Many trips spend one night there or on Isla del Sol.
- **To or from Peru**: the lake is the usual overland link between La Paz and Puno, and on to Cusco. The Kasani–Yunguyo border crossing is near Copacabana. See [combining Bolivia with Peru](/questions/combine-bolivia-and-peru-in-two-weeks/).

The lake is at a similar height to La Paz. Arriving from La Paz after a few days there adds little extra altitude strain.
