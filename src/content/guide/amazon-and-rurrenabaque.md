Rurrenabaque is a river town on the Beni River in the northern lowlands, at roughly 230 m. It is the usual base for trips into Madidi National Park and the pampas wetlands.

## Getting there

- **By air**: short domestic flights from La Paz. Weather and the condition of the runway can cause delays or cancellations, especially in the wet season.
- **By road**: the road from La Paz through the Yungas is long. Journey times vary widely with the weather and road works.

## Jungle or pampas

- **Madidi (jungle)**: lodges and camps upriver inside or near Madidi National Park, reached by motorised canoe. Walks in rainforest, birds and plants. Some lodges are run by or with local communities.
- **Pampas**: trips to the wetlands of the Yacuma river area, where open water makes wildlife such as caiman, capybara and birds easier to see from boats.

Many visitors do both, usually three days each.

## Health in the lowlands

The Amazon is low, hot and humid. The health rules for the lowlands are different from the highlands:

- CDC recommends yellow fever vaccination for travellers to areas below 2,300 m east of the Andes, which includes the whole of Beni department.
- CDC lists malaria transmission in areas below 2,500 m.

Speak to a travel clinic well before you go. See [entry and health](/guide/entry-and-health/).
