The Cordillera Real runs north-west to south-east along the edge of the Altiplano, above La Paz and Lake Titicaca. It holds many peaks above 5,000 m and several above 6,000 m.

## Main peaks

- **Huayna Potosí, about 6,088 m**: close to La Paz and often climbed as a guided 2–3 day ascent with a high camp. It is a glaciated climb: crampons, ropes and a guide are needed.
- **Illimani, about 6,438 m**: the mountain on La Paz's skyline. A longer, more serious expedition.
- **Chacaltaya, about 5,395 m**: a former ski area. A road reaches close to the top, so it is a common day trip and an acclimatisation test.

Bolivia's highest mountain, Nevado Sajama at about 6,542 m, is in a separate range to the south-west, in Sajama National Park.

## Trekking

Multi-day treks cross the range between the Altiplano and the Yungas valleys, often on old paved trails. Common choices include the Choro, Takesi and Yunga Cruz trails, which descend from passes above 4,000 m into the cloud forest. Longer treks run along the range around Condoriri and the Illampu–Ancohuma massif near Sorata.

## Acclimatisation

Spend several days at La Paz's height before trekking, and longer before climbing. Mountaineers often build up with lower peaks or treks before a 6,000 m summit. Clinical guidelines on rate of ascent are summarised on the [altitude ladder](/altitude/).
