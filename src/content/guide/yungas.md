The Yungas are the steep, forested valleys on the eastern slope of the Andes, between the high Altiplano and the Amazon lowlands. From La Paz the road climbs to La Cumbre, at around 4,650 m, and then drops thousands of metres into warm, humid valleys.

## The Yungas road

The old road from La Cumbre to Coroico was once known for its fatal accidents. A newer road now carries most traffic, and the old route is used mainly by downhill mountain-bike tours. The descent is long and the drops beside the track are severe. Choose an operator with well-maintained bikes, helmets and support vehicles.

## Coroico

Coroico, at about 1,525 m, is a small town on a ridge above the valleys. After La Paz it is warm, green and easy to breathe in, so many visitors use it for a few days of rest after the bike descent or a trek.

## Health

The Yungas are much lower than La Paz. CDC advice for Bolivia applies different vaccine and malaria guidance to areas below certain heights. See the [entry and health page](/guide/entry-and-health/) and check with a travel clinic.
