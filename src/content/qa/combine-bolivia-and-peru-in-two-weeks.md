## A two-week outline

| Days | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Cusco and the Sacred Valley | Acclimatise. The Sacred Valley is lower than Cusco. |
| 4–5 | Machu Picchu | By train via Aguas Calientes. |
| 6–7 | Puno and Lake Titicaca (Peru) | By road or train from Cusco. |
| 8–9 | Copacabana and Isla del Sol (Bolivia) | Cross the border at Kasani–Yunguyo. |
| 10–12 | La Paz | City, Tiwanaku, Valle de la Luna. |
| 13–14 | Uyuni or fly home | See below. |

## Adding the salt flats

The 3-day Uyuni and Lípez trip does not fit comfortably into two weeks with Machu Picchu. Your options:

- **A one-day salar trip**, flying La Paz–Uyuni and back.
- **Drop a Peru stop** to make room for the full 3-day trip.
- **End in Chile**, with the 3-day trip finishing at San Pedro de Atacama, if your flights home allow it.

## Altitude

Cusco is at about 3,400 m, so it is a high start in its own right. Spending the first days there and in the lower Sacred Valley means you reach Titicaca and La Paz already partly acclimatised. See the [altitude ladder](/altitude/).

## Border and paperwork

Check entry rules for both countries for your nationality, and make sure you are stamped out of Peru and into Bolivia. See [entry and health](/guide/entry-and-health/).
